Logo Usage

✓ Do

Use the logo on clean, uncluttered backgrounds. Maintain adequate white space around the logo.

✓ Do

Use the primary color palette (Hex Blue #00A8E8 on dark backgrounds, Dark #0D1117 on light backgrounds).

✗ Don't

Don't stretch, rotate, or distort the logo. Always maintain original proportions.

✗ Don't

Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds or low-contrast environments that reduce visibility.
