# OmnyraGroup Brand Guidelines
## Version 1.1 — Premium GRC Training & Risk Advisory

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Who We Are
OMNyra Group is a premium global security training and risk advisory firm. We bridge the critical gap between dry academic theory and real-world executive operations. We focus exclusively on **GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance)**, **Third-Party Risk**, **ISO 27001 readiness**, and **regulatory compliance**.

### 1.2 Our Mission
To prepare ambitious students and corporate professionals to build lucrative, sustainable careers globally through active practitioner-led lectures, intensive 1-on-1 career strategy coaching, interactive software sandboxes, and rigorous panel mock interviews — transforming trainees into highly sought-after GRC specialists.

### 1.3 Brand Pillars
- **GRC Excellence** — Governance, Risk & Compliance at the highest level
- **Practitioner Authority** — Taught by those who've operated in the field
- **Career Transformation** — From student to specialist, from theory to practice
- **Global Reach** — Careers without borders, expertise without limits
- **Premium Precision** — Every detail reflects the standards we teach

### 1.4 Brand Promise
"We don't teach cybersecurity. We forge GRC specialists."

## 2. Logo System

### 2.1 Primary Logo — The Sentinel Mark

**Orbital Emblem — The Sentinel Ring:**
- Segmented chrome ring = the continuous monitoring cycle of GRC discipline
- Four directional notches = the four core domains (GRC, Third-Party Risk, ISO 27001, Regulatory Compliance)
- Blue gradient circle = the knowledge core — alive, radiant, protected
- White ascending arrow = career trajectory, the launch from trainee to specialist
- Chrome nodes = the four stakeholder touchpoints (Student, Trainer, Employer, Industry)
- Dashed orbital ring = the invisible but ever-present compliance framework

**Wordmark:**
- **OMN** — Bold/ExtraBold, Pure White — the unbreakable foundation
- **yra** — Light/Regular, Electric Blue — precision, the trained eye
- **GROUP** — Medium, Silver, tracked wide — global scale, corporate authority

### 2.2 Logo Variations

| Variation | Use Case |
|-----------|----------|
| Primary (Horizontal) | Website header, business cards, proposals, formal documents |
| Stacked | Social media profiles, certificates, square formats |
| Icon Only | Favicons, app icons, watermarks, security seals |
| Monochrome White | Dark backgrounds, single-color print, engraved materials |
| Monochrome Dark | Light backgrounds, embossing, formal print |

### 2.3 Clear Space
Minimum clear space = height of "O" in "OMNyra" on all sides. The security perimeter is never breached.

### 2.4 Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide (primary), 48px (icon)
- Print: 30mm wide (primary), 12mm (icon)

## 5. Logo Usage Rules

### 5.1 Permitted
- Deep Space bg → Full color
- White bg → Monochrome dark
- Photography → Deep Space overlay 70-85%
- Electric Blue bg → Monochrome white only
- Midnight bg → Full color (preferred elevated dark)

### 5.2 Prohibited
- Do not rotate, stretch, or distort
- Do not add effects (shadows, glows)
- Do not change color ratios
- Do not recreate in other typefaces
- Do not rearrange emblem/wordmark
- Do not animate the emblem
- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1

### 5.3 Security Seal
For certificates and credentials:
- Icon emblem + circular text path
- "CERTIFIED BY OMNYRAGROUP"
- Electric Blue on Deep Space
