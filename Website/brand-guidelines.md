| **Monochrome White** | `Monochrome_white-transparent.png` | Dark | Dark backgrounds — business card front, dark social covers, brand guidelines header |

### Logo Usage Rules

#### DO

- Use the logo on clean, uncluttered backgrounds with adequate white space
- Use Hex Blue (`#00A8E8`) on dark backgrounds, Dark (`#0D1117`) on light backgrounds
- Maintain minimum clear space around the logo (at least 1x the logo height on all sides)
- Use the correct variant for each background context

#### DON'T

- Don't stretch, rotate, or distort the logo — always maintain original proportions
- Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds or low-contrast environments
- Don't use low-resolution versions of the logo
- Don't add effects (drop shadows, outlines, gradients) to the logo

## 5. Brand Applications

### Business Cards

- **Size:** 3.5 × 2 inches (standard US)
- **Front:** Dark gradient background (`#0D1117` → `#161B22`), Monochrome White logo (top-left), name + title (bottom-left), icon-only watermark in `#0077B6` at 15% opacity (bottom-right)
- **Back:** White background, Primary logo (centered), contact details with Hex Blue icons, accent bar at bottom, tagline at bottom-center
- **Accent bar:** 3px, gradient `#00A8E8` → `#0077B6`

### Letterhead

- **Size:** 8.5 × 11 inches (US Letter)
- **Header:** Primary logo (left), contact info (right), blue accent line below
- **Side accent:** 4px left edge, vertical gradient `#00A8E8` → `#0077B6`
- **Watermark:** Icon-only logo, bottom-right, 4% opacity, 200px
- **Footer:** Contact icons + tagline, 1px top border

### LinkedIn Cover Banners

- **Dimensions:** 1584 × 396 px
- **Dark theme:** Gradient background, hex grid pattern (6% opacity), white mono logo, headline + subheadline + CTA button
- **Light theme:** Light gradient background, hex grid pattern (4% opacity), primary logo, headline + subheadline + CTA button
- **Logo position:** Left-aligned with headline text, 50px height, 16px top margin
- **Watermark:** Icon-only, right side, vertically centered, 320px, 8% opacity
- **Bottom accent:** 4px, full-width gradient bar
- **CTA Button:** Hex Blue (`#00A8E8`), 12px × 28px padding, rounded 6px, uppercase, white text

### Social Post Templates

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1080 px
- **Dark theme:** Dark gradient, hex grid, white mono logo, quote content, hashtags, CTA
- **Light theme:** Light gradient, hex grid, primary logo, quote content, hashtags, CTA
- **Logo position:** Top-left, 50px height
- **Watermark:** Icon-only, right-middle (vertically centered), 280px, 6% opacity
- **Bottom accent:** 4px, full-width gradient bar
- **CTA Button:** Hex Blue, 14px × 32px padding, rounded 8px

### Email Signature

- **Layout:** Stacked logo (100×100px, no circle) + name + title + gradient divider + contact grid + social icons + tagline
- **Width:** 600px max
- **Contact icons:** Hex Blue (`#00A8E8`), 14×14px
- **Social icons:** LinkedIn + Twitter, 32×32px, light Hex Blue background
- **Divider:** 2px, gradient `#00A8E8` → `#0077B6`
- **Tagline:** Uppercase, 10px, Chrome Lattice (`#8B949E`), letter-spacing 1.5px

## 6. Visual Motif

### Hexagonal Lattice / Network Pattern

The brand uses a hexagonal grid pattern as a recurring visual motif representing structure, connectivity, and the GRC framework network.

- **Pattern:** Repeating hexagon outlines forming a honeycomb lattice
- **Stroke:** 0.5px, brand accent color
- **Opacity:** 4-6% on light backgrounds, 6% on dark backgrounds
- **Usage:** Background texture on social covers, posts, letterhead, and digital assets
