Questions about the AI Governance track

Do I need a technical background for the AI Governance track?

No coding is required. A basic understanding of GRC concepts helps, so some learners pair this track with the GRC track or take it after Lattice.

Is the training live or recorded?

Live. Every session is taught online by a practitioner in real time, with time for questions and case work. There are no pre-recorded lectures.

How many hours of training do I get?

That depends on the plan you choose: Node is 10 hours, Lattice is 20 hours and Nexus is 35 hours. Nexus also includes all six tracks.

What will I have built by the end?

Construct an Enterprise AI Risk Register with impact scoring, conduct a comprehensive Algorithmic Impact Assessment for a GenAI deployment, and formulate an ISO 42001-aligned AI Acceptable Use Policy.

Do you help with finding a job?

Yes. Lattice and Nexus include ATS-friendly resume rebuilding, LinkedIn profile updates, a mock interview and ongoing career mentorship. Nexus adds 1-on-1 executive coaching and priority placement support.

Is a certification exam included?

You receive an OMNyra certificate of completion. External certification exams and their fees are not included. If you plan to sit AIGP, ask on your free call how this track maps to it.

Is there a discount for groups?

Yes. Friends or colleagues enrolling together as a group of 3 or more get group pricing, and organisations can book a dedicated cohort with custom scheduling.
